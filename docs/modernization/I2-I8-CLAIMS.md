# Portfolio claim policy for I2-I8

## Claim supported by the repository now

> Designed a Pega client-managed platform architecture for OpenShift covering licensing/entitlements, vendor image governance, Artifactory-style quarantine/approval, Helm integration, multi-environment promotion, platform dependencies, IAM/security and GitOps release management.

## Claims not yet supported

Do not state:

- “Pega Infinity 26 deployed on my CRC”;
- “Pega production HA validated”;
- “Pega upgrade/rollback tested”;
- “Pega Customer Service runtime demonstrated”;
- “Pega CDH/Constellation runtime validated”;
- “official Pega image successfully pulled”.

Those claims require actual authorized runtime evidence.

## Future upgrade of claim

Once official entitlement is available and the deployment succeeds, attach evidence to the exact Pega release, image digests, chart ref and OpenShift environment before changing the claim to `RUNTIME VALIDATED`.
